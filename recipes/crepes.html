Crepes Recipe

Home

This is an easy and fast crepes recipe perfect for anytime.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
    Steps
  1. In a mixing bowl, whisk together the flour, eggs, milk, melted butter, sugar, and salt until smooth.
  2. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and lightly grease with butter.
  3. Pour 1/4 cup of the batter into the skillet, swirling to coat the bottom evenly.
  4. Cook for 1-2 minutes until the edges start to lift, then flip and cook for another minute.
  5. Repeat with the remaining batter, stacking the cooked crepes on a plate.
  6. Serve warm with your favorite fillings and toppings. Enjoy!
