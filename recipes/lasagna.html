Lasagna Recipe

Home

This is a delicious lasagna recipe that is easy to make and perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
    Steps
  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef, onion, and garlic over medium heat until the meat is browned. Drain any excess fat.
  3. Add the marinara sauce to the skillet and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. In a mixing bowl, combine the ricotta cheese, egg, and half of the Parmesan cheese. Mix well.
  5. Spread a thin layer of the meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  6. Layer 3 lasagna noodles over the sauce, then spread half of the ricotta mixture on top.
  7. Add a layer of mozzarella cheese, then repeat the layers (meat sauce, noodles, ricotta, mozzarella).
  8. Finish with a final layer of noodles, meat sauce, and the remaining mozzarella and Parmesan cheese on top.
  9. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  10. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 15 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and golden.
  11. Let it cool for 10 minutes before serving. Enjoy!
